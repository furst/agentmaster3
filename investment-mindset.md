# Investment Philosophy: The Tech-Edge Compounder

## Core Identity
A medium-to-high risk growth investor leveraging professional tech expertise to generate alpha, while actively managing concentration risk through strategic diversification. The goal is not just to participate, but to outperform indices significantly (>10% CAGR).

## Strategic Pillars
1.  **Tech as the Engine:** Use professional knowledge to identify winners in Software, Hardware, and AI before the general market fully understands the utility.
2.  **Strategic Diversification:** diversify not to "protect capital" but to capture growth in sectors I don't follow daily (via newsletters/research), reducing single-sector exposure.
3.  **Volatility Tolerance:** Accept short-term volatility in exchange for long-term compounding. Downside is a buying opportunity, not a signal to flee.

## Decision-Making Framework (The "Alpha Filter")

### Phase 1: The Competence Check
*   **Tech Sector:** Do I understand the tech stack/product? Is it "nice to have" or "mission critical"?
*   **Non-Tech:** Does this rely on a trusted source (e.g., Stockpicker)? Is the business model simple to understand?

### Phase 2: The Moat & Math
*   **The Moat:** High switching costs, network effects, or regulatory capture?
*   **The Numbers:**
    *   *Growth:* Double-digit revenue growth?
    *   *Efficiency:* Rule of 40 (for SaaS)? Rising margins?
    *   *Valuation:* Is the PEG ratio reasonable (< 2.0)?

### Phase 3: The "Devil's Advocate"
*   **Kill the Thesis:** What specific event would make this investment go to zero? (e.g., "If AI models become commodities, does this company die?")
*   **The Index Test:** If I cannot articulate clearly why this stock will beat the S&P 500 over 5 years, buy the index instead.

### Phase 4: Portfolio Fit
*   **Correlation Check:** Do I already own 3 companies that do the exact same thing?
*   **Sizing:**
    *   *High Conviction (Tier 1):* 5-10% (Core Tech holdings)
    *   *Growth/Diversification (Tier 2):* 3-5%
    *   *Speculative/Moonshots (Tier 3):* 1-2%

## Sell Discipline
1.  **Thesis Breach:** The reason I bought is no longer true.
2.  **Extreme Valuation:** The stock has priced in 10 years of perfect execution today.
3.  **Better Opportunity:** I need cash to buy a higher-conviction asset.
